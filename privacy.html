Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

About the app

n8n secretary is a personal application. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to any other users.

Data we access

The app requests access to the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events. This access is used only to read events and to create events in the owner's own Google Calendar.

How data is processed

All data is processed exclusively within the owner's own n8n instance. The data is not shared with third parties, is not sold, and is not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings, under Security, in the section for third-party apps with account access.
